    Before you drive

    The plain rules

    Who can drive

    Drivers aged 21 and over with a current licence. Bring your licence and passport or ID to the hand-over — tell us where your licence was issued when you book and we will confirm what you need to drive here.

    Fuel and kilometres

    Kilometres are unlimited — Betio to Bonriki as often as you like. Return the tank as you got it, or add prepaid fuel to your quote and hand it back at any level.

    Bond and damage

    A refundable bond is held at hand-over and returned when the car comes back in the same condition. Salt, sand and coral dust are our problem, not yours — we wash every car after a hire.

    If something goes wrong

    • Call us first, any hour, on 7305305 or 73039089.
    • We come to you anywhere on South Tarawa.
    • If we cannot fix it on the spot, we swap the vehicle.
    • Days you lose to a breakdown are not charged.
