    Before you drive

    General information

    Who can drive

    Drivers aged 21 and over with a current licence. Bring your licence and passport or ID to the hand-over. Tell us where your licence was issued when you book and we will confirm what you need to drive here.

    Fuel

    Return the tank as you got it. If you would rather pay for a full tank up front and bring the car back at any level, tell us when you book.

    Damage and compensation

    We do not hold a bond. You and Tenana Rentals sign a hire contract at hand-over, and that contract sets out how any damage or loss is compensated. Read it before you sign and ask us about anything that is not clear.

    If something goes wrong

    Call 73053005 or 73039089 at any hour. We come to you anywhere on South Tarawa, and swap the vehicle if we cannot fix it on the spot.
